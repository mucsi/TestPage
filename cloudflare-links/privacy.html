Expo Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Laserox Design Kft. handles personal data when you use Expo Quest, join its daily prize draws, redeem rewards or contact us about the app. It covers the visitor app and the associated event administration. It does not replace the separate policy for purchases or newsletter subscriptions on laserox.net.

Who is responsible

Laserox Design Kft. is the controller of Expo Quest participant data and the organizer of its daily draws. You can contact us at Dózsa György út 105, 1224 Budapest, Hungary, or info@laserox.net. Please mention Expo Quest when contacting us about privacy.

What information we handle

We handle your chosen explorer name, optional email address, event identifier, completed challenges and quests, stars earned and spent, draw entries, reward claims and approval records. Records can include timestamps and the staff identifier associated with a reward approval. We also record the privacy-notice version acknowledged and notification-permission status when available.

The app generates a pseudonymous participant identifier from a hashed device identifier where the operating system supplies one, or from a random value otherwise. This identifier links your progress and event records. It is not anonymous when it can be linked to your email or other information, and reinstalling the app is not a reliable way to erase or change server records.

For fairness checks, the app reports the highest observed rate of star collection and the difference between earned stars and the available maximum. The app synchronizes participant and gameplay data even if you choose to play without an email address. Offline changes can be uploaded when connectivity returns.

The camera is used to read challenge and reward-approval QR codes. Expo Quest processes camera frames on the device and does not upload those frames as part of participant synchronization. QR contents used to identify challenges or approve a reward can be processed as gameplay or redemption data. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, microphone recordings or payment-card details. A completed challenge can nevertheless indicate which event activity you visited.

When you contact us, we receive the contents of your message and your contact details. If you win a prize that needs shipping, we arrange delivery by email and request the recipient name, delivery address and any contact details needed for that shipment. Shipping details are not required to play quests.

Our hosting and delivery services receive technical connection information, such as an IP address, requested resource, request time and client information, when the app downloads content, follows a challenge link or contacts the backend.

Why we use the information

We use participant and gameplay records to operate quests, calculate stars and entries, approve rewards, avoid duplicate redemptions, operate draws and contact winners. Providing an email is optional for quest play but necessary to participate in daily draws and receive prize correspondence.

Our legal bases are performance of the participation service you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, and our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) in operating a secure and fair event and understanding participation. Where consent is required for a particular use, we must obtain it separately under Article 6(1)(a). Acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent. Legal obligations are handled under Article 6(1)(c).

We use first-party participation statistics to understand which challenges are used and improve the event. We do not use your draw email for newsletters or advertising, sell participant data, or give exhibitors or prize providers participant details for their own marketing. Promotional banners shown in the app are event content, not permission to add you to a marketing list.

Daily draws and choices

Providing an email gives one draw entry. Each earned star adds an entry, including quest bonuses. Spending stars does not reduce entries. Draw selection is random and weighted by the recorded entries: more entries give a greater chance of selection. Winners are excluded from further prizes that day and eligible again on following days. The event's draw information explains dates, prizes and collection arrangements.

A winner's explorer name may be announced publicly. We do not announce the winner's email publicly. Staff use the supplied email to contact the winner and arrange delivery where needed. If you believe a draw or fairness decision is incorrect, contact us for a human review.

You can remove your email through Help and leave the daily draws. The app hides your earned entries and removes the one email bonus; adding an email later restores eligibility and that bonus. Email removal is not the same as deleting all participant records: saved draw results or administrative records may still contain your previous information. For broader deletion, contact info@laserox.net.

Camera and notification permissions can be changed in device settings. Notifications are optional. Without camera permission, the app cannot scan QR codes. Clearing local app data or uninstalling the app does not itself delete records already held on our servers or staff devices.

Children and families

There is no minimum age for participating in the event activities. A parent or legal guardian should provide their own email address and manage draw participation, prize contact and shipping for a child. Please do not enter a child's email address or include unnecessary personal details in an explorer name.

This arrangement does not mean the app verifies age or parental responsibility. Parents can contact info@laserox.net to review, correct or request deletion of information relating to their child. Where the law requires parental authorization, it must be obtained before the relevant processing; access to event activities is not itself authorization to use a child's personal data.

Who receives information

Authorized LASEROX staff and administrators can access the records needed to operate the event, approve rewards, run draws and answer requests. The admin app can keep local records while offline and synchronize them later.

Supabase provides our participant database, backend services and staff authentication. The participant database is currently configured in its London region. Cloudflare delivers event content and challenge-link pages. GitHub is used to publish the content and host the editor; services accessed through GitHub can receive technical request information. Participant emails and private draw records are not intended for publication in the public content repository.

Email and delivery providers receive the information necessary to handle correspondence or deliver a prize. These are service-related disclosures, not permission for their independent marketing. We may also disclose information when required by law or to address a specific legal claim. Access should be limited to what the recipient needs.

Provider information is available at https://supabase.com/privacy, https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/ and https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. A provider's own notice supplements, but does not replace, our responsibilities for participant data.

International processing

The London database is outside the European Economic Area. The European Commission currently recognizes the United Kingdom as providing an adequate level of protection. Other provider operations, support and technical services can involve additional countries; database location alone does not guarantee EU-only processing.

Transfers requiring safeguards must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Contact info@laserox.net to request information about the providers and transfer safeguards applicable to your data, including how to obtain a copy.

How long information is kept

Our maximum retention period for participant email addresses and identifiable daily-draw records is one calendar month after all prizes for the event have been delivered. This limit also applies to saved draw pools, results and organizer-controlled exports containing that information. Information no longer needed should be removed earlier, including following a valid erasure request.

Gameplay progress, entries, star balances and claimed-reward progress are event-specific and are cleared manually by the organizer before the next expo. This manual event reset is separate from the one-month privacy deadline and is not a substitute for deleting personal data from other copies. Locally cached information can remain on a device until the app receives the reset or the device's app data is cleared.

Correspondence, shipping and other administrative information is kept only for as long as needed to resolve the request, complete delivery or meet a specific legal obligation. Technical security logs are retained only as necessary for service operation and security under the relevant service arrangements. Truly anonymous totals may be retained because they no longer identify participants.

If a specific law requires us to retain a particular document longer, we restrict it to that legal purpose rather than retain the entire participant profile for convenience. Ask us for the applicable reason and period. This is not an extension of ordinary draw participation or marketing use.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for backend communication. We restrict staff access and use safeguards appropriate to the service. No system is completely secure. Please avoid putting sensitive information in your explorer name or unsolicited messages, and protect access to your device and email account.

Your rights and how to contact us

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. Contact info@laserox.net with your explorer name, event and relevant email so we can locate your records. We may ask for proportionate verification, but do not send identity documents unless requested and necessary. We normally respond within one month and explain any lawful extension.

You may complain to your local data-protection authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, NAIH: 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, Hungary; https://www.naih.hu. You do not have to contact us first to exercise that right.

Policy updates

We update this policy when the app or our processing changes and show the revision date above. Material changes will be brought to your attention as appropriate. Using an updated notice is not a waiver of your rights or permission for unrelated new uses of your data.
