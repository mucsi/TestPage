Expo Quest

Request deletion of your data

You can ask Laserox Design Kft. to delete your Laserox Expo Quest participant data at any time. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in.

Request deletion by email

This opens your email app. Review and send the message to submit your request. If the button does not work, email info@laserox.net with the subject Expo Quest data deletion request.

What to include

  • Your explorer name and the expo you attended.
  • The email address you provided in Expo Quest, if any. If possible, send your request from that address.
  • If you played without an email, say so. We will help locate your records and may ask for proportionate verification.

A parent or guardian can make a request for their child. Do not send passwords, identity documents or reward-approval QR codes.

What happens next

Our team reviews your request, verifies ownership where necessary and arranges deletion. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is necessary, we will explain it. Clicking the email button alone does not submit a request or delete anything.

What data the request covers

A full deletion request covers your identifiable participant profile, explorer name, email address, participant identifier, challenge and quest history, stars earned and spent, daily-draw entries and identifiable draw and reward-claim records, including organizer-controlled copies where applicable.

Deletion removes your participation records and daily-draw eligibility. Tell us about any outstanding prize delivery so we can explain how deletion affects it.

Retention and exceptions

Under our privacy policy, participant emails and identifiable daily-draw records are retained for no more than one calendar month after all prizes for the event have been delivered; valid deletion requests can lead to earlier removal. Event progress is cleared manually before the next expo. You do not need to wait for that reset to request deletion.

If a specific legal obligation requires a particular record to be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period and restrict its use to that purpose. Truly anonymous totals may remain. Correspondence needed to process your request is kept only as necessary to resolve it or meet a specific legal obligation.

Data saved on your phone

Uninstalling the app or clearing its local data does not delete server records. Similarly, a request does not remotely erase every offline copy on your phone. Mention any devices you still use; our team will advise how to clear local data and avoid uploading old progress again.

Only want to remove your email?

In the app, open Help and select “Delete email address”. This leaves the daily draws but is not a request to delete all participant data.
