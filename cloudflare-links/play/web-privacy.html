About your web account

The web backup is separate from the installed Expo Quest apps. It stores your chosen name, contact email, account identifier, event progress and reward claims. Supabase provides password authentication; a private internal sign-in identifier is derived from your email. Your actual contact email is not verified and no verification or recovery emails are sent.

We do not store your password in browser storage. The browser stores your session, event content and progress so you can return and play during connection interruptions. Protect access to your device and keep your recovery code private. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted by your device; reconnect regularly to synchronize.

If you choose daily draws, your contact email and synchronized entries are available to event administration. Turning off daily draws removes your email from the participant draw profile, but keeps it as the sign-in identifier of your web account. To delete the account itself, use the data-deletion request below.

Landing-page statistics count page launches grouped by day, page type and broad device category. They do not identify unique people. The counter does not store your email, account identifier or challenge token. A temporary hash of connection information is used to limit abusive counting; hosting providers also process normal connection and security logs.

The controller, contact details, purposes, retention periods and data-rights information are in the Expo Quest privacy policy. Web account and authentication records must also be included when processing a deletion request.
