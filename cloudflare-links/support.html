Expo Quest

How can we help?

Get help with Laserox Expo Quest, your companion for exploring participating expos, collecting stars and discovering rewards.

Email Expo Quest support

Contact info@laserox.net. If the button does not open your email app, copy this address into your email service. No account is needed to contact us.

What to include

Describe the problem and include the expo name, phone model, operating system and app version if known. A screenshot may help. Do not send passwords, identity documents or reward-approval QR codes. Check screenshots for personal information before sending.

Getting started and loading content

The first launch requires internet access to download event content. Complete setup and choose an explorer name. Email is optional for quests and challenges. If loading is slow, try a reliable Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection. After a successful download, cached content and core progress can work offline. Reopen the app online to check for updated content.

Keep your progress safe

Avoid uninstalling the app or clearing its storage when troubleshooting: progress is saved on your phone, and reinstalling does not guarantee recovery. Contact us first if progress appears missing.

Scanning challenge QR codes

Tap SCAN and allow camera access. Keep the full code visible, improve lighting and avoid glare. If permission was denied, enable camera access for Expo Quest in your phone settings. Each challenge awards stars only once. If a code is not recognized, ask booth staff to check that it belongs to an enabled challenge for the current event.

Claiming physical rewards

Select an available reward you can afford and start a claim. Show your claim QR to LASEROX staff. They approve it in the separate Admin app; then scan their approval QR to finish. Approval codes are specific to each claim. If a claim fails or looks incomplete, ask staff before starting another one.

Daily draws and email

Open a daily-giveaway card to view prizes and event information. An email is required so winners can be contacted. Providing email adds one entry; each earned star adds one entry. Spending stars does not reduce entries. Connect to the internet before a draw so your entries can synchronize. Follow the event's published eligibility rules and schedule.

To remove a provided email, open Help and choose “Delete email address”. This leaves the daily draws but does not delete all progress. A parent or guardian should handle children's email and prize contact.
